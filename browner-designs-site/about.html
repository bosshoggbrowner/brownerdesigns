About

From the classroom to the screen.

Same goal either way: take something complicated and make it make sense.

B.S.Secondary Ed. — Biological Sciences, Clemson

Hi, I'm Ryan.

I have a Bachelor of Science in Secondary Education: Biological Sciences from Clemson University. I started out teaching — standing in front of a classroom and figuring out, day after day, how to explain things clearly enough that they'd actually stick.

That turned into a career in instructional design and e-learning, and eventually into Browner Designs: a small design studio built around the same core skill. Whether I'm laying out a homepage, mapping an app's onboarding flow, or storyboarding a training course, I'm asking the same question I asked as a teacher — will this actually make sense to the person on the other end?

I'm passionate about style, creativity, gaming, and technology, and I like combining all of it with a genuine design and education background. All designs are created in-house, by me, in the U.S.A.

Outside of client work, you'll usually find me experimenting with new design tools or playing through whatever game inspired my last project idea.
