A quick summary

I hold a Bachelor of Science in Secondary Education: Biological Sciences from Clemson University and a Master of Business Administration from Louisiana State University Shreveport. I started out teaching, then followed my passion into learning and development, with a focus on technology — instructional design and e-learning development, building courses and training programs for real learners with real deadlines.

Founding Browner Designs was a natural next step: taking that same instructional-design thinking and applying it to websites and apps, where clarity and usability matter just as much as they do in any training program.
